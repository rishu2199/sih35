## 1. Executive Summary & Core Impact on SIH Problem Statement 26035

### What is GATC?
A **Government Approved Test Centre (GATC)** is an authorized laboratory (public or private) recognized under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 to undertake verification and stamping of weights and measures.

### Crucial Relevance to Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments (NAWI):
1. **First Schedule Expansion:** The amendment officially substitutes the **First Schedule [Rule 3(1)]**, expanding the list of instruments that GATCs are legally authorized to verify to **23 categories**.
   - **Item 6:** Non-automatic weighing instruments of **Accuracy Class III (upto 150kg)**.
   - **Item 7:** Non-automatic weighing instruments of **Accuracy Class IIII**.
   - **Item 8:** **Load cell** (the fundamental transducer in electronic NAWIs).
   - **Item 9:** Beam scale.
   - **Item 10:** Counter machine.
   - **Item 11:** Weights of all categories.
2. **Fifth Schedule (Verification Fees):** The amendment adds 14 new fee categories (Items 19 to 32) specifying official statutory verification fees in Rupees (₹), including fuel dispensers, automatic weighing instruments, taximeters, etc.
3. **Software Pitch Power:** In our presentation, our application should feature a pre-built profile for **"GATC Testing and Verification Workspace"**, automatically populating verification fees, verifying compliance up to 150kg Class III, and generating the statutory verification certificate!
